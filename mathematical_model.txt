====== Mathematical model and notation ======

===== Ambient space and surface =====

The ambient space is Euclidean R^3. Let D be a supported subset of R^2, with coordinates (u,v). A surface patch is

<code>
r : D -> R^3
r(u,v) = (x(u,v), y(u,v), z(u,v))
S = r(D)
</code>

The regular case requires r to be sufficiently differentiable and r_u x r_v != 0. Piecewise-regular charts and seams are allowed only where their behavior is explicitly modeled. A plane is the special case r(u,v)=(u,v,0).

D is the primary computational domain. Regions and patterns are defined in D, then mapped to S. The software must distinguish a parameter point from its physical image because r may not be injective.

===== Surface metric =====

Let r_u=partial r/partial u and r_v=partial r/partial v. The first fundamental form is

<code>
g = [[E,F],[F,G]]
E = r_u dot r_u
F = r_u dot r_v
G = r_v dot r_v
|g| = E*G - F*F
</code>

A parameter-space algorithm uses ordinary (u,v) coordinates. A surface-aware algorithm uses g and intrinsic operators. These modes must be declared explicitly; uniform parameter spacing does not imply uniform physical spacing.

For scalar p, the Laplace-Beltrami operator is

<code>
Delta_S p = (1/sqrt(|g|)) partial_a (sqrt(|g|) g^(ab) partial_b p)
</code>

with repeated a,b summed over u,v. Implementations must define singularity and regularity assumptions before evaluating this expression.

===== Time-dependent partition =====

At time t, active regions are P(t)={Omega_i(t): i in I(t)}. I(t) may change over time. For a full partition, region interiors are pairwise disjoint and their closures cover the domain, subject to a documented boundary-ownership convention. A background/unassigned region can be used if full coverage is not required.

Geometry changes include boundary motion and deformation. Topology changes include birth, death, split, merge and adjacency changes. These are distinct operations.

===== Region representations =====

Possible region representations include polygonal faces in a shared-edge complex, spline boundaries, level-set functions phi_i(u,v,t), and discretized labels. No one representation is assumed suitable for all algorithms. The authoritative first-release representation is a shared-edge planar subdivision; alternatives must be converted/validated before they become committed topology.

For an implicit region defined by phi<0, the boundary is phi=0. Regular level-set points require grad(phi) != 0. Independent level sets do not by themselves guarantee a valid partition; they must be reconciled.

===== Fields and generators =====

A region's generator may maintain state q_i. The state may be a scalar/vector field, discrete site states, geometry primitives, particles, or another typed representation. Do not identify generator state with rendered output.

A general update may be written as

<code>
q_i(t + dt) = G_i(q_i, Omega_i, boundary_data, parameters, random_state)
</code>

This is a conceptual contract: static generators, continuous-time solvers, discrete automata and stochastic generators have different stepping semantics. The interface should reflect those differences through capabilities and typed contexts.

===== Derived parameter coordinates =====

A generator may define psi_t(u,v)=(h(u,v,t), k(u,v,t)). If a pattern is defined in the derived coordinates by p_tilde(h,k,t), its original-coordinate representation is

<code>
p(u,v,t) = p_tilde(h(u,v,t), k(u,v,t), t)
</code>

The Jacobian is [[h_u,h_v],[k_u,k_v]]. A singular Jacobian, non-injective mapping, or absent inverse may be valid for some methods and invalid for others; validity requirements must be declared by the generator.

===== Interface conditions =====

Adjacent regions i and j share an interface Gamma_ij(t). Conditions may match field values, fluxes, derivatives, phases, curve endpoints, directions, minimum clearances, or a soft compatibility objective. The meaning of any normal derivative or flux must define which metric and orientation it uses.

Hard constraints are required to satisfy a stated numerical tolerance. Soft constraints contribute an objective or penalty and may remain nonzero. Infeasibility and unsupported conditions must be observable outputs, not silently hidden.

===== Time domains =====

Distinguish geometric time, generator time, and render/display time. A continuous solver may substep within one display frame; a cellular automaton advances in integer generations; topology events occur at specified simulation times. The scheduler owns ordering and synchronization policy.
